# Notification Service

## Purpose and status

PLANNED capability for authorized notifications to patients, clinicians, researchers, hospital administrators, and governance reviewers. No provider, protocol, runtime, or delivery code exists.

## Responsibilities and owned concepts

Potential notification-event intake, preference/routing coordination, delivery status, and provider adaptation.

## Communication boundaries

Consumes explicit event contracts and delivers through future provider adapters; sensitive payloads must be minimized.

## Non-responsibilities

It does not own clinical decisions, patient records, authorization policy, or source business events.

## Expected contracts

PLANNED notification event, recipient preference, delivery request/status, and provider-error contracts.
